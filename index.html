The Maison

Every object earns
its weight in metal.

Founded by a single goldsmith with one bench and one loupe, BEVORA still tests every alloy by hand before it becomes a piece worth keeping. Nothing leaves the atelier that we would not wear ourselves.

14 Years at the Bench
32 International Honours
100% Recycled Precious Metal
0 Pieces Rushed

Hand-Set Stones

Every gem is seated under a loupe, one prong at a time, by a single setter from start to finish.

Alloyed In-House

We melt and test our own metal blends, tuning colour and hardness batch by batch.

Traceable Sourcing

Every gram of gold and gram of carbon is logged from mine or lab to finished piece.
